# 1. Product Overview

FWorld is a premium ecommerce platform focused on selling men's fashion products directly to customers across India.

The platform aims to provide a luxury shopping experience while maintaining fast performance, modern UI, enterprise security, and excellent SEO.

The website must be mobile-first, highly scalable, and future-ready for expansion into women's fashion, kids' fashion, mobile applications, and AI-powered shopping.
